# Attenda — Business Overview

*For corporate readers: hotel owners, GMs, and partners. This guide explains what Attenda does, what it costs, how money flows, and how to run it day to day — without any technical detail. For system setup and developer reference, see the full technical manual.*

---

## 1. What Attenda Is

Attenda replaces the outdated tools hotels still run on — binders, whiteboards, radios, printed menus, group texts — with one system the whole property shares.

**Four audiences, one platform:**

- **Hotel operators** — every department coordinated in real time, with a data trail on every guest interaction
- **Guests** — scan a QR code in the room; no app download, works on any phone
- **Vendors & partners** — a simple tablet dashboard to receive and fulfill orders
- **Attenda** — an owner-level command center across every property, live at all times

**The number that matters:** hotels using a system like this cut front-desk call volume by up to **60%** — fewer interruptions, faster answers, and a service record if there's ever a dispute.

---

## 2. What the Hotel Team Gets

One dashboard, organized by department:

- **Front Desk** — guest requests come in as tickets (not phone calls), each tracked from received to done, nothing lost between shifts
- **Housekeeping** — enter today's rooms (checkouts, stayovers, do-not-disturb) and the system instantly calculates the crew needed, including breaks
- **Maintenance** — preventive maintenance schedules + repair requests, each room tile shows red if anything is open
- **Transportation** — the shuttle board, live vehicle tracking, seat bookings
- **Food & Beverage** — breakfast covers and waste tracking in one place
- **Security** — daily activity reports and patrol logs, digitally filed
- **Manager views** — scheduling, revenue reports, labor productivity, service recovery

**Every number the property reports on Monday comes out of this system automatically.**

---

## 3. What the Guest Gets

1. Scan the QR code in the room (or type a short link)
2. Enter a first name — no account, no download
3. The phone becomes the room's control panel for the whole stay:

- Order food from hotel outlets and local partner restaurants
- Book the shuttle or request a custom ride
- Message the front desk
- Get WiFi, checkout time, and property info instantly
- Leave a review
- Local deals and partner offers from businesses around the hotel

**If anything goes wrong** — a bad review, a complaint — it opens a service case automatically. The manager sees every open case and closes the loop with the guest before they check out.

---

## 4. What Vendors & Partners Get

- **Restaurants, transport companies, experience providers** get a free tablet dashboard: orders arrive live, they tap through received → preparing → ready → delivered
- They manage their own menu, prices, photos, and hours
- They see exactly what they're owed on every order — Attenda's fee is itemized, never hidden
- The hotel promotes them to guests at no cost to be listed

## 5. Running More Businesses on Attenda (White-Label)

Any business — a restaurant, a vendor, a partner brand — can run its **own** storefront as a page of Attenda: `attendaapp.com/theirname`.

What they get:

- **Their own branded site** — their colors, their words, their products
- **Their own store** with their menu, prices, and photos
- **Their own admin page and password** — separate from every other business
- **Orders delivered to their WhatsApp** — checkout by chat, where their customers already are

Three ways to start:

1. **Ask for a demo** — Attenda builds their real, branded store with their menu. The demo is the actual product, not a mockup.
2. **Self-serve** — they answer a short wizard online and get a working store in minutes.
3. **White-glove** — Attenda sets everything up hand-in-hand with their team.

**Demo stores run free for 24 hours.** Once they sign, the same store flips to permanent with zero rework — their menu, photos, and orders all carry over.

This matters for a hotel group too: your outlets, spas, or partner businesses can all live as branded pages with separate logins under one platform.

## 6. The Money: Fees & Revenue Sharing

### What Attenda charges partners

| Partner type | Attenda fee | Partner keeps |
|---|---|---|
| Restaurant | 10% | 90% of the order |
| Experiences | 10% | 90% of the booking |
| Services | 0–5% | 95–100% (negotiated) |
| Transport | 10% | 90% of the ride |

### Card processing (separate, at cost)

- **3%** on credit card transactions
- **0%** on debit and prepaid cards

### Example: a $100 restaurant order

| | |
|---|---|
| Guest pays | $100.00 |
| Attenda commission (10%) | −$10.00 |
| Card fee (3%) | −$3.00 |
| **Vendor receives** | **$87.00** |

Of Attenda's $10, the hotel can receive a share (typically up to 50% of Attenda's portion, agreed per partner).

### What this means for a hotel

- Partner sales through the platform = **new revenue with zero new work** — the restaurant brings its own tablets and staff, the hotel just hosts the storefront in front of its guests
- Shuttle: free hotel shuttle stays free; paid routes can charge per seat with payment handled by the platform
- Every order is recorded permanently — guest charge, vendor payout, fees — clean books at any audit

## 7. The Daily & Weekly Rhythm

**Every shift change** — outgoing staff confirm their tickets are closed or handed over (one screen, two minutes)

**Every morning** — the manager enters the day's room counts; housekeeping gets its crew plan instantly

**Monthly admin review** — five minutes on: labor vs plan, service recovery cases, shuttle usage, partner revenue. All graphs are pre-built; nothing to export or calculate.

**When a guest complains** — the case lands in Service Recovery automatically. The staff member follows up; the manager closes it. Every step is time-stamped, so "we handled it" is now provable.

---

## 8. Getting a New Property Live

A new property goes live with a **10-minute checklist**, run once:

1. Enter the day's real room counts → verify the crew numbers look right
2. Log one test shift → confirm it reads back correctly
3. File one test maintenance ticket → see it on the room
4. Submit one test guest request → confirm it arrives
5. Close out the day → confirm every counter matches what was entered

If all five behave, the property is live. Anything unexpected, Attenda support steps in before the team starts using it for real.

---

## 9. Housekeeping Labor: The One Rule Worth Knowing

Attenda plans crews with a fixed, tested productivity model, applied the same way at every property:

- **30 minutes** per checkout clean
- **15 minutes** per stayover service
- Each maid contributes **420 minutes of actual cleaning per 8-hour shift** — the rest of the shift is lunch, breaks, cart setup

The system does this math automatically the moment room counts go in. Rooms marked out of order are held back automatically until maintenance returns them to service.

## 10. Answers to the Questions Owners Ask

**"Is guest data private?"** — Each business's data is isolated from every other's; one property can never see another's.

**"What if a guest doesn't finish onboarding?"** — No account is needed; the phone becomes the room's control panel for the whole stay.

**"What does Attenda cost the hotel?"** — The platform fee is set in the Attenda agreement. Partner commissions and card fees come out of partner sales, not the hotel's pocket.

**"Do we need new hardware?"** — No. Staff use any computer, tablet, or phone. Guests use their own.

**"What happens with staff turnover?"** — New staff get their own login day one; departing staff lose access same day. Nobody shares passwords.

**"Can we run our sister properties the same way?"** — Yes — every property gets the full system with its own data, and ownership sees all of them from one command center.
